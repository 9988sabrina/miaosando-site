幕后：怎么把真猫做成 AI 剧情主角Behind the scenes: how we make an AI cat

  1. ① 真猫照片1 · Real photo

    从三朵的真实照片出发，每次生成都带上它做参考。Every scene starts from real photos of Miao Sando as reference.

  2. silver-white chinchilla British Shorthair, round chubby face, large steel-blue eyes, pink nose, thin royal-blue collar…

    ② 固定角色设定2 · Locked character sheet

    毛色、眼睛、蓝项圈永远不变，保证每一集都是同一只猫。Fur, eyes and the blue collar never change — same cat, every episode.

  3. ③ 生成关键帧3 · Key frames

    按分镜逐个生成写实电影感画面，暖色灯光、浅景深。Cinematic frames, shot by shot, from a storyboard.

  4. ④ 图生视频 + 剪辑4 · Animate & edit

    每个镜头做成 5–8 秒动画，配旁白、音乐，剪成 60 秒小剧场。Each frame becomes a short clip; voice-over and music make a 60-second story.
