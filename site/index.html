故事 · The StoryThe Story

她在为未来奋斗，
它在为她“顺便”。She's chasing her future.
Her cat is "just passing by."

主人是一个在美国读大学的女生。赶 due、刷题、图书馆坐到闭馆，再一个人走夜路回家——喵三哆是她在异国他乡唯一的家人。

它从不承认自己在等门。只是碰巧趴在书桌上，碰巧把台灯旁的位置焐热，碰巧学会了煮面。每一集，都是主人看不到的那些“顺便”。

Her human is a college student in the US — deadlines, problem sets, the library until closing, then the walk home alone. Far from home, Miao Sando is her only family.

She'll never admit she's waiting up. She just happens to nap on the desk, happens to keep the lamp-side spot warm, happens to learn how to cook noodles. Every episode is one of those quiet favors her human never sees.
